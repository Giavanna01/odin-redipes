Chocolate Cake

Discrption

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Cocoa powder
  • Baking powder
  • Baking soda
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Oil
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla extract
  • Boiling water

Steps

  1. Wisk flour, suger, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large mixing bowl until well blended
  2. Add in milk, oil, eggs, and vanilla. Then while mixing add the water slowly over time
  3. Bake for 30-35 minutes
  4. Wait for 10 minutes then frost
